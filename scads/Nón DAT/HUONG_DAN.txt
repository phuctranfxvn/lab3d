NON LUOI TRAI - BO FILE OPENSCAD
=================================

FILE TRONG THU MUC
  non_day_lai.scad   file chinh, mo bang OpenSCAD
  than_non.stl       than non, mat ngoai vung co chu da lam phang (bo duong chi
                     may, nep vai) de chu in sac net hon
  than_non_goc.stl   than non nguyen ban tu file 3MF, khong sua gi (de doi chieu)
  Baloo2-ExtraBold-Static.ttf   font

TRUOC KHI MO
  Cai font truoc, neu khong OpenSCAD se thay bang font mac dinh va chu sai het.
    Windows  chuot phai file TTF > Install
    macOS    mo bang Font Book > Install Font
    Linux    chep vao ~/.local/share/fonts roi chay  fc-cache -f
  Giu than_non.stl va file font cung thu muc voi file .scad.

DOI CHU
  Sua 2 dong dau trong phan Noi dung chu. Go dau tieng Viet binh thuong.
  Giu nguyen co_dong1 = 39.35 cho moi ten. Thong so nay la CO CHU chu khong
  phai be rong, nen ten dai ngan gi thi chu van cao bang nhau.
  Panel truoc chi chua duoc khoang 300 mm be ngang. Ten dai tren 12 ky tu
  thi giam co_dong1 xuong, moi 10% giam la be rong giam 10%.
  Tham chieu: co_dong1 = 39.35 thi "Thay Doan" rong 255 mm, "Thay Hoang" rong 284 mm.

DANG CHU
  om_cong   true = chu om theo mat cong, giu dung be rong chu that tren non.
            Nhin thang tu truoc, chu o hai ben se hep lai, nhu chu theu that.
  do_noi    do noi cua chu so voi mat non (mm kich thuoc goc, mac dinh 4).
            Non mini (ti_le ~0.1): 3-5. Chu thap thi it keo to, canh duoi it xe.
  bo_tron   ban kinh bo tron mep chu (mac dinh 0 = thanh thang, dinh phang, de in nhat).
            Chi nen bo tron 2-3.5 khi in to (ti_le tu 0.3 tro len).
            Lon hon nua thi chu tron day hon, nhung net manh se nhon dinh.
  so_lop    so lop tao phan bo tron (mac dinh 10). Moi lop chi ~0.1 mm khi in
            o ti_le 0.4 nen nhin lien mach.
  Mat non duoc luu dang luoi cao do o cuoi file .scad (LUOI_W), dung sua.

XUAT FILE IN
  Nhan F6 roi File > Export > Export as STL. Ket qua chi co phan CHU.
  Than non co dau % nen chi hien trong xem truoc, khong nam trong file xuat ra.
  Day la co y: mesh goc co 5739 manh roi va 64832 canh ho, OpenSCAD khong
  boolean duoc voi no. Cach lam: nap chu.stl vua xuat cung voi than_non.stl
  vao slicer. Hai file cung he toa do nen tu khop, khong can can chinh.
  Chon loai_non truoc khi xuat:
    Non Ecotek       tu dung ti le 0.4 x 29.09% = 0.11636 (~97 mm be ngang)
    Non Toan Phuong  tu dung ti le 0.4 x 27.9%  = 0.1116  (~93 mm be ngang)
    Tuy chinh        dung ti_le tu nhap.
  xoay_tu_the_in (mac dinh bat): file xuat ra da xoay san tu the in
    X -3.35, Y 58.66, Z 86.86 do (giong "Rotate (absolute)" trong Orca).
    Non ngua ra sau, mat chu huong len tren nen chu in thanh cac lop tren cung,
    sac net nhat. Nap vao slicer khong can xoay lai.

DO CHINH XAC
  Than non: chi cac dinh mat ngoai trong vung chu (|u| < 170, |v| < 92 mm
  quanh giua mat truoc) duoc dua ve mat min, chuyen tiep dan trong 20 mm o
  mep. Mat trong, hong non, luoi trai giu nguyen.
  Chu va than non dung chung mot mat min nen chan chu khop sat mat non.
  Trong vung chu, mat ngoai than non trung khit voi mat ma chu bam theo.

THOI GIAN RENDER
  OpenSCAD ban 2021 dung CGAL: khoang 3 phut tren may 1 nhan.
  OpenSCAD 2023 tro len dung Manifold: nhanh hon nhieu lan.
